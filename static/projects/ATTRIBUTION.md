# Project illustrations

The project visuals are synchronized from the Andesprit catalog. They are user-commissioned AI-generated concept illustrations, not screenshots of product interfaces or evidence of independent product testing. Nineteen purpose-led illustrations retain the full composition and headings in the original card frames. Flow Atelier retains its separately approved workflow illustration and original 1586×992 dimensions.

The OpenAI, Anthropic and Grok marks in the Flow Atelier concept were requested by the owner. They do not imply a partnership or endorsement. Source-code license labels are established separately from artwork and remain linked to their upstream evidence.

The original portrait, system typography and other pre-existing portfolio assets retain their original roles.
